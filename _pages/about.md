I am **Yuxuan Peng**, a master's student in Petroleum Engineering working at the intersection of drilling engineering and computational mechanics.

My research focuses on the **multibody dynamics simulation of articulated flexible drillpipe**. I use multibody dynamics modeling, numerical simulation, and data analysis to study load transfer, pipe orientation, contact behavior, and vibration in drillstring systems operating in complex wellbores. I am also interested in developing reproducible engineering tools for model verification and design optimization.

### Research Focus
* **Multibody Dynamics Simulation of Articulated Flexible Drillpipe:** Modeling articulated drillpipe systems to investigate coupled friction, contact, joint-limit, and vibration effects.
* **Drillstring Mechanics:** Analyzing load transfer, orientation evolution, and wellbore contact in horizontal and complex well trajectories.
* **Numerical Simulation and Data Analysis:** Using Python, NumPy, pandas, and visualization tools to extract engineering-relevant load and motion characteristics from simulation results.

### Selected Project
* **Articulated Flexible Drillpipe Analysis:** An analysis of axial force, torque, joint orientation, and wellbore contact loads in ultrashort-radius horizontal wells. [View project](/portfolio/)
